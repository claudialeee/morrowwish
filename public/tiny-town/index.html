How do clubs work? (optional)

Your turn: Put down one venue each week. You get 8 moves.

Neighbors: Each has different interests and a walking range (2–4 steps).

Clubs: Different neighboring venues form a club when both host at least two people for two consecutive weeks.

Victory: By Week 8, get 9 neighbors out of the house and form 2 lasting clubs.

Advanced: capacities, scoring & mechanics
Seats & upgrades

Arcade 3 · Music Studio 2 · Art Workshop 2 · Café 2. Optional upgrades add one seat.

Resident choices

Neighbors prefer primary over secondary interests and pick venues automatically, considering distance and availability. Each attends one venue per week. Rocks block paths. Adjacent means sharing an edge, not a corner.

Different club types

Put different venues side by side; each needs 2 visitors for 2 consecutive weeks.

Vitality & grades

Each week: +10 for each active resident, +24 per stable club, +6 per forming club, +4 per attended venue type, −5 per empty venue. Total scores of 1,000/1,150 earn A/S on winning; other wins B, losses C.

Controls

Hover over a tile to preview. On mobile, tap a tile twice to confirm. Tap Residents & interests to inspect everyone, including the four upcoming arrivals. New maps vary their layouts and offers.
