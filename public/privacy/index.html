MORROW WISH · THE LITTLE DETAILS

Privacy & Play

Our browser games are free to play. No account, download or payment is needed. Here's what happens when you visit or play.

What we collect

  • First-party site and gameplay analytics: page visits, game starts, completions, retries and other in-game progress signals.
  • Visit and browser identifiers, referral website or campaign tags, approximate country, and device type. A random browser identifier is stored in local storage to help count returning visitors, unless your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Some games save choices and progress in your browser's local storage so you can return to them.

We don't require your name or email address to play. If you email us, we'll receive the information you choose to send.

Why we use it

To understand which games people try, where they get stuck, and what to improve. We use aggregated metrics to evaluate and develop the games.

Your choices

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable the persistent analytics visitor identifier. Basic page-visit and gameplay events can still be counted. You can clear saved game progress and browser identifiers by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings.

Hosting and other platforms

The site is delivered through Vercel and Cloudflare, which may process technical request data as part of hosting. If you follow a link to itch.io or another service, that service's own privacy practices apply.

Studio-owned social accounts

In the private Studio Insights dashboard, authorized studio operators can grant read access to studio-owned social accounts. We use platform-issued tokens to read the approved campaign videos and their available metrics, such as views, likes, comments and shares. Tokens are encrypted in server storage and metrics are cached for the private dashboard. Browser-game players do not need to connect a social account. The account owner can revoke access in the platform's connected-app settings or email us to request deletion of stored authorization data.

Questions?

Contact morrowwishgames@gmail.com.

MORROW WISH · 关于游玩与隐私

隐私与游玩

我们的网页游戏可以免费游玩，无需账号、下载或付费。以下说明你访问和游玩时会涉及哪些信息。

我们会记录什么

  • 网站和游戏的第一方统计：页面访问、开始游戏、完成、重玩，以及部分游戏进度事件。
  • 访问及浏览器标识、来源网站或推广参数、大致国家和设备类型。除非浏览器启用了“请勿追踪”（DNT）或“全球隐私控制”（GPC），否则网站会在本地存储中保存随机浏览器标识，用于了解回访情况。
  • 部分游戏会把选择和进度保存在你的浏览器本地存储中，方便再次游玩。

游玩无需提供姓名和邮箱。如果你主动发邮件联系我们，我们会收到你选择提供的信息。

为什么记录

我们希望了解大家尝试了哪些游戏、在哪里遇到困难，以及哪些内容值得改进；游戏开发决策主要参考汇总统计。

你的选择

启用 DNT 或 GPC 后，网站不再保存持久的统计访客标识，但基本访问与游玩事件仍可能计入统计。你可以通过浏览器设置清除本站的本地存储，删除本地游戏进度和访客标识。

托管与其他平台

网站使用 Vercel 和 Cloudflare 提供服务，这些服务可能为了网站托管处理必要的技术请求信息。如果你点击前往 itch.io 等其他平台，相关平台适用各自的隐私规则。

工作室自有社交账号

获得授权的运营人员可在私有看板连接工作室自有社交账号，授权读取已批准的宣传片及平台提供的播放、点赞、评论和分享等指标。授权令牌经加密后存放在服务器，读取结果仅缓存供私有看板使用。玩家无需关联任何社交账号。账号所有者可以在对应平台的已连接应用设置中撤销授权，也可以通过邮件申请删除已保存的授权数据。
