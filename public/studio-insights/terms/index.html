Morrow Wish Studio Insights Terms

Studio Insights is Morrow Wish's private, read-only reporting dashboard. It measures the performance of the studio's own game promotions alongside activity on its browser-game site.

Account access

Only an authorized studio operator may connect an account the studio manages. The account owner authorizes access through the platform's own consent screen and can revoke access from the connected-app settings on that platform. The dashboard can read eligible promotion data. It cannot upload, edit or delete social posts.

Availability

Metrics are subject to each platform's reporting, permissions and availability. Missing metrics may be left blank, and cached metrics show the time they were last retrieved.

Contact

For access or data requests, email morrowwishgames@gmail.com. See the Morrow Wish privacy policy.
