A LETTER FROM ELSEWHERE

Dear you,
another life is waiting.

The bookshop by the sea has ten days left on its lease. The old cinema in Paris closes on Sunday. Footprints cross a frozen lake, and no one knows where they lead. At the greenhouse, Bea wants to play her violin, while her neighbor just wants somewhere quiet to sit.

That's how these stories begin. No one knows quite what to do next, but somehow the decision falls to you.

Some doors won't close once you've opened them. Some places become better after you leave.

We'll stop writing here. The rest is yours to live.
