# CAT BOND — THE LAST RESONANCE
## Main Story Script · Canon 1.2 · full player-first campaign rewrite

Authoritative scene-by-scene playable narrative. Canon 1.1's approved opening direction is preserved; all six chapters, finale, epilogue and postgame are rewritten. Locked world facts and ending remain unchanged. ZH / EN lines are paired, gameplay directions are implementation requirements, not subtitles. This is a complete critical-path script, not proof of runtime migration or a full ambient-dialogue database.

## PROLOGUE — A NAME TO FIND / 序章·寻找名字

**Presentation lock:** no opening animation, historical montage, flashback, mandatory slideshow, or image of the former Bellkeeper and Muling together. Show one optional text overlay on the live garden, dismissible immediately; then give control. History is not a playable flashback until Chapter V/VI. The following Chinese/English pairs are authored opening dialogue; gameplay directions are not dialogue.

### P-01 — The world, in a few lines / 世界的背景
**Optional text over live scene (no character images):**
- ZH: 很久以前，六片土地的猫咪曾依靠一座古老的星铃维系记忆。一场灾难击坏了星铃，猫咪们开始忘记熟悉的地方、朋友，甚至自己的名字。灾难曾经平息，生活也渐渐恢复。直到最近，失忆的现象再次出现。而今天，你在一座陌生的花园里醒来。
- EN: Long ago, an ancient Star Bell helped the cats of six lands preserve their memories. A disaster damaged the bell, and cats began forgetting familiar places, friends, even their own names. The crisis subsided and life returned to normal. Recently, the forgetting began again. Today, you wake in an unfamiliar garden.

**Action:** dismiss or skip once; control immediately. No cinematic completion or mandatory lore reading.

### P-02 — A stranger in the garden / 独自醒来
**Action:** fixed golden-shaded cat wakes alone in Sunlit Garden. No companion or companion HUD. Move freely; chase a butterfly, investigate flowers, find a shell, hear a faint bell. Show a small current objective, not a compulsory chain of tutorial dialogs.
Player: “这里是哪里？……我的名字呢？怎么一点也想不起来？” / “Where am I? …My name? Why can't I remember it?”
Player: “先看看附近有没有认识我的猫吧。” / “I'd better see if anyone nearby recognizes me.”

Optional sign, **阳光花园·旧铃纪念碑 / Sunlit Garden · Memorial Bell**:
“多年前，一颗坠落的星辰击坏了星铃。许多猫咪因此忘记了家园和亲友。愿每一只迷路的猫，都能找到回家的路。” / “Years ago, a falling star damaged the Star Bell. Many cats forgot their homes and loved ones. May every lost cat find its way home.”
Inspecting the old bell makes it answer once; no identity label or explanation. Player can leave without inspecting it. The garden's exact historical ownership remains hidden.

### P-03 — A map on the wind / 风中的航图
**Action:** a windblown map catches on a low branch; an unfamiliar pale silver-shaded traveling cat is trying to retrieve it. The player can help by approaching and freeing the map while she steadies the branch. She introduces herself only after the event. If ignored, preserve the opportunity to meet at the harbor.
Stranger: “等等！那张图可不能拿去做鸟窝！” / “Wait! That map is not nesting material!”
Player: “这是你的？” / “Is this yours?”
Muling: “谢啦。叫我暮铃吧。我是个到处找古铃、修旧东西的旅行者。” / “Thanks. I'm Muling. I travel around looking for old bells and fixing things.”
Player: “你认识这附近吗？我连自己的名字都记不起来。” / “Do you know this area? I can't even remember my name.”
Muling: “最近不只你这样。霁潮港也有猫突然忘了熟悉的人和路。那里有座古铃，我正准备去看看。” / “You're not the only one lately. Cats in Misttide Harbor have been forgetting people and roads they know. There's an old bell there I'm going to investigate.”
Muling: “我得先去还一件工具。港口见——如果你也想去的话。” / “I need to return a tool first. See you at the harbor, if you're heading that way.”
**Exit:** she leaves independently. No recruitment, forced intimacy or plot-dependent reward for optional help.

### P-04 — The road to the harbor / 去港口
**Action:** player can explore garden and its surroundings before taking the path. An unreachable crevice and unusual distant light are optional future exploration hooks, not mandatory tasks. A sign points to the harbor; a passing cat mentions the lighthouse trouble. Optional map marker: 去霁潮港，寻找认识我的猫 / Visit Misttide Harbor and look for someone who knows me.
Player: “也许那里能找到我的过去。先去看看吧。” / “Maybe I'll find something about my past there. I'll go and see.”
If P-03 was skipped, the harbor encounter introduces Muling naturally; never pretend the player already knows her.

### P-05 — At the harbor gate / 港口入口
**Action:** arrive independently. If the map encounter occurred, Muling recognizes the player from that meeting; otherwise she introduces herself as a traveling researcher using P-03's public identity lines. Do not show evidence of their older relationship. She is here for the broken bell and the player is here for a personal lead. They are not yet bonded.
Muling (met before): “航图没再飞走，算是个好兆头。” / “The map hasn't flown away again. That's a good sign.”
Muling (not met): “你好，我叫暮铃。来调查港口那座坏掉的古铃。” / “Hello, I'm Muling. I'm here to investigate the harbor's damaged old bell.”
**Exit:** player explores the harbor; H-01 contains the incident and invitation to temporary cooperation. No companion state until accepted. A refusal permits continued exploration and later acceptance without replaying the incident.

## CHAPTER I — MISTTIDE HARBOR / 第一章·回家的灯
### 归潮调 / Returning Tide

**Player promise:** Find a lead about my own past while bringing a lost boat home. Start with an inviting market, a practical emergency, then a storm adventure. Harbor and garden remain freely revisitable. Introduce 栖蓝 through action; 岬灯 appears at the lighthouse. Unnamed residents use existing ambient cats, not new major cast assets.

### H-01 — A boat without a way home / 找不到家的船
**Enter:** independent harbor arrival; if P-03 was skipped, introduce Muling at P-05. The player can explore before accepting the rescue.
**Play:** market fish stalls, shells, a tide cave visible at low tide. A fisher repeatedly walks past their own doorway; 栖蓝 ties a distinctive blue cloth to the door and walks them home. A boat offshore answers a bell from the wrong direction.
渔夫: “船我认得。可下了船……我该往哪儿走？” / “I know my boat. But once I'm ashore… where do I go?”
栖蓝: “看，蓝布那扇门。您家门口的鱼干还是昨天晒的呢。” / “See the blue cloth? That's your door. Your fish are still drying outside.”
Player: “原来不只我记不起过去。” / “So I'm not the only one who's forgotten.”
Muling: “多年前星铃坏了，记忆就开始消失。最近又出现了。我来查港口的古铃。” / “When the Star Bell broke years ago, memories began to disappear. It's happening again. I'm investigating the old bell here.”
栖蓝: “等等，那艘船已经第三次绕过礁石了！” / “Wait! That boat has circled the reef three times!”
Muling: “我有绳子。你愿意帮忙吗？” / “I've got rope. Want to help?”
**Exit:** accept temporary cooperation or defer; deferral leaves Muling at dock and exploration available. First objective is rescue, not collecting a fragment.

### H-02 — Two lights / 两盏灯
**Play:** climb lighthouse path, compare real beacon housing and false glow over reef; 岬灯 physically stops an unsafe switch activation. Let player inspect a tide chart before choosing immediate shore assistance or preliminary beam calibration; each has a distinct operation and later support consequence. No irreversible harm on a failed minigame.
岬灯: “别点灯。灯罩偏了半尺，船会往礁石上开。” / “Don't light it. The lens is off by half a foot. It'll send boats onto the reef.”
栖蓝: “那我先把外面的船带到安全水域。” / “Then I'll guide that boat into safe water.”
岬灯: “你留在岸上。” / “You're staying ashore.”
栖蓝: “你不能每次都替我选。” / “You can't decide for me every time.”
Player: “先看看能让船听见我们的办法。” / “Let's see how we can get a signal to the boat.”
**Exit:** safe approach selected, basement opened. Optional challenge: return a drifting package, no plot gate.

### H-03 — Salt on the logbook / 盐渍航海日志
**Play:** explore dry and flooded passages; tides expose routes, paired lever holds a gate, mirrors rotate the safe beam. Recover the rescue log from a marked chest, not a random drop. Three short excerpts establish what 岬灯 did and that the crew survived. A shell trail also leads to an optional shy cat.
岬灯: “我那年关了灯，想拦住出海的船。救援船却早就出去了。” / “I turned the light off that year to keep boats ashore. The rescue boat had already left.”
栖蓝: “所以他们找不到回来的方向？” / “So they lost their way home?”
岬灯: “是。船员活了下来，可我从此不敢再把航线交给别人。” / “Yes. The crew survived, but I haven't trusted anyone else with a route since.”
栖蓝: “那这次你看灯，我看海。我们谁也别替谁做决定。” / “Then you watch the light and I'll watch the sea. Neither of us decides for the other.”
**Exit:** 岬灯 agrees to test her chart, not blanket forgiveness; physical beam alignment verified. Muling checks an old fitting in the background, innocuous on first play.

### H-04 — The bell in the fog / 雾里那声铃
**Play:** Muling anchors rope; player pulls in safe wave windows. 栖蓝 navigates while 岬灯 guides. A mist creature mimics a ship's bell and retreats into darkness. Boss phase 1: dodge wave cones and activate two lamps together to expose it. Phase 2: maintain the true signal while defeating the exposed echo; no damage-only victory. Failure returns to safe shore checkpoint. The battle is a rescue, not a detached arena.
岬灯: “左边是礁石。光路给你了，航线你选！” / “Reefs to port. The light is yours; choose your route!”
栖蓝: “看见了！跟真正的灯走！” / “I see it! Follow the real light!”
Muling: “我守这盏，你去另一边。三、二——就是现在！” / “I'll hold this lamp; take the other. Three, two—now!”
After docking, Muling tries to wring out her soaked map; player may help or tease her.
Muling: “你找过去，我查古铃。看来我们挺适合一起迷路的。先同行一段？” / “You're looking for your past; I'm investigating old bells. We seem rather good at getting lost together. Shall we travel together for a while?”
Player: “好啊，不过下次把图收好。” / “Sure. But keep your map somewhere safe next time.”
**Exit:** actual rescue and mutual acceptance establish companion status; paired sensing/traversal unlocked. If player defers permanent companionship, allow free exploration and return to accept before the next chapter; do not silently recruit.

### H-05 — The first note / 第一个音
**Play:** the repaired lighthouse reveals its sanctuary. The player instinctively starts a note; local cats voluntarily answer 归潮调, Muling joining the second phrase a little early. After restoration, fog lifts locally; walk through the market and see the fisher recognize the blue door and a familiar neighbor. Some memories still missing. Use the earned paired action to reveal a hidden tide-cave passage; optional cat, souvenir, garden return.
Player: “我从没学过这首歌，为什么知道怎么开头？” / “I've never learned this song. Why did I know how to start it?”
Muling: “也许以前听过。剩下的得让这里的猫来唱。” / “Maybe you've heard it before. The cats here have to sing the rest.”
栖蓝: “归潮调！船回港的时候，我们就唱这个。” / “Returning Tide! We sing it when boats come home.”
**Exit:** harbor melody and repair verified separately. 栖蓝 sends a supply boat to forest with a request for medicine; a reply from forest must actually return before reciprocal link is complete. The shipment has a message: forest's medicinal plants are wilting. Player's next lead: an old traveler record in the forest may identify their tune.
**Reward:** harbor route, optional cats, paired action, souvenir and visible homecoming. Player can revisit safely.

## CHAPTER II — ROOTSLEEP FOREST / 第二章·会走动的森林
### 相枝调 / Living Roots

**Player promise:** A living maze, a mystery solvable by observing the environment, and a local crisis that makes the first regional exchange matter. Muling is now a companion, not a quest narrator. Primary local actor 芽禾; 榆川 encountered underground after investigation.

### R-01 — The missing medicine / 没有送到的药
**Enter:** arrive with Harbor's supply message. Before exposition, see a delivery basket hanging upside down on a root, a dry herb bed, and a tiny glowing trail disappearing into foliage.
**Play:** follow a scent trail using proximity, wind and visual pawprint hints; player may climb a canopy detour to find an optional wild cat. The forest paths change when roots swell. 芽禾 demonstrates how to read fresh leaves and introduces herself while retrieving the basket.
芽禾: “糟了，这一筐药再晒下去就没用了！港口还等着呢。” / “Oh no, these herbs will spoil in the sun! The harbor's waiting.”
Player: “这里刚下过雨，为什么药园干成这样？” / “It just rained. Why are the herb beds so dry?”
芽禾: “地下那边说根没水，可我们的水也不见了。你看，泥地上有两条水痕。” / “The root keepers say they're dry, but our water is gone too. Look, two wet trails.”
Muling: “我追右边那股湿气，你可以先看看左边。走丢了就喊我。” / “I'll follow the damp trail on the right. You can look left. Call if you get lost.”
**Exit:** choose to stabilize upper herb beds or underground roots first (distinct trust and resources; no permanent loss), find the medicine route rather than accuse a suspect.

### R-02 — Under the roots / 树根下面的世界
**Play:** follow scents into hidden tunnels. A missing shipment is found being used to treat diseased roots; investigate three physical clues (medicine wrapping, root sickness, a diverted water pipe) in any order. 榆川 opens a small gate only after seeing evidence. Player discovers an old travel journal, but the name is washed away; its notation matches the first note.
榆川: “药是我拿的。根再烂下去，上面的树也活不了。” / “I took the medicine. If the roots rot, the trees above won't survive either.”
芽禾: “你要药可以告诉我！我们还以为有人偷水。” / “You could have asked me! We thought someone was stealing water.”
榆川: “我把水偷偷分回药园，没想让你们担心……结果两边都不够。” / “I quietly diverted water back to the herb beds so you wouldn't worry… now neither side has enough.”
Player: “先把水路画出来。不然我们每次都只能猜。” / “Let's map the channels first. Otherwise we'll always be guessing.”
Optional interaction: Muling fails to identify a harmless mushroom and 芽禾 laughs; later she brings a correctly labeled seed to garden. Do not turn this into another main quest.
**Exit:** shared channel diagram unlocked. The old traveler record is evidence the melody predates current crisis, not a name reveal.

### R-03 — When the forest moves / 森林在移动
**Play:** a root collapse separates the upper and lower paths. Player directs a partner to hold pressure while moving between three readable physical controls, water pressure, gate and roots. 芽禾 distributes water above, 榆川 holds the lower gate. An echo copies a familiar guiding whistle and moves false markers; real scent/water direction distinguishes it. Battle and rescue integrated: interrupt the echo to free obstructed roots; not a generic damage gate. Retry checkpoint retains solved safe operations. 
芽禾: “药园先别管！下面还有猫没出来！” / “Forget the herb beds for now! There are cats still down there!”
榆川: “我撑住闸门，你们把水往左引！” / “I'll hold the gate. Send the water left!”
Muling: “我在这里。看水痕，别追那个声音。” / “I'm right here. Follow the water marks, not that voice.”
**Exit:** both communities visibly safe, shared channel opened; player learns paired traversal/scent-reading. No automatic trust from repeated lever presses.

### R-04 — The answer beneath the leaves / 树叶下的回声
**Play:** both forest communities answer 相枝调; water begins flowing to both gardens. Collect the promised medicine into the returning harbor shipment and confirm receipt when Harbor reports it. Choose a seed to bring home; paired traversal opens a previously unreachable canopy perch.
Player: “那块石头为什么画了两条路？” / “Why are there two paths carved into that stone?”
Muling: “一条通中央星铃，另一条连着隔壁的圣所。那条旧路断了很久。” / “One leads to the Star Bell. The other connects to the next sanctuary. That old route's been broken for a long time.”
Player: “如果修好，两个地方就能互相听见了？” / “If we fix it, can the two places hear each other again?”
Muling: “得有人在另一头回应才行。” / “Only if someone answers at the other end.”
**Visible payoff:** dry plants recover, bridge roots reopen, medicine returns to harbor. 芽禾 and 榆川 post a joint water map instead of magically agreeing on everything.
**Hook:** a forge-bound cooling-water shipment needs a new pump; an old metal plate bears the same two-route diagram. Deliver the forest's actual offer to Emberforge; forge must return pump/tools to complete the link. Player wants to identify the symbol and help the forest.

## CHAPTER III — EMBERFORGE CITY / 第三章·熄火的节日
### 承火调 / Carry the Flame

**Player promise:** a lively festival with playful competitions before a public technical crisis; a credible choice about responsibility, not another identical resource dispute. 小铆 is the early contact, 烬婆 enters when testing becomes dangerous.

### E-01 — A festival without fire / 没有火花的庆典
**Enter:** forest delivery. The player arrives during a lantern-making fair; the central furnace sputters and stops midway through a demonstration. Warm workshop sounds, food and practice targets before urgency. Optional rhythm-forging contest has repeatable score but no main-story grind.
**Play:** catch a rolling part, help 小铆 assemble a portable lantern, test it on a small safe bench. Forest needs a replacement pump.
小铆: “小心！那是我的泄压阀——呼，接得漂亮！” / “Careful! That's my relief valve—whew, nice catch!”
Player: “你能用它做水泵吗？” / “Could you use it to make a water pump?”
小铆: “当然，可主炉又卡住了。我的新设计能自动泄压。” / “Of course, but the main furnace jammed again. My new design vents pressure automatically.”
烬婆: “收起来。没验证过的东西不准接主炉。” / “Put it away. Nothing untested goes into the main furnace.”
小铆: “旧图纸也不是永远正确的！” / “The old plans aren't always right!”
**Exit:** get access to test workshop. Muling shows a comically misshapen practice lantern; player may keep it as a garden souvenir.

### E-02 — The missing signature / 被擦掉的签名
**Play:** compare two blueprints against actual furnace geometry; recover accident report from a locked safety drawer through mechanical assembly, not theft. A damaged pressure gauge and deadline notation prove the old design was commissioned without a full test. Two workers were injured. 烬婆's signature is visible, not erased by magic.
Player: “这里写着测试没做完。谁让炉子提前开的？” / “It says the test wasn't finished. Who authorized the early startup?”
烬婆: “我。庆典等着，我以为不会出事。” / “I did. The festival was waiting. I thought nothing would go wrong.”
小铆: “我不是想证明师傅错了。我只想知道这次怎么不再伤到别人。” / “I'm not trying to prove you wrong. I want to know how we keep anyone else from getting hurt.”
**Choice:** share verified safety findings immediately, or ask 烬婆 to tell workers herself before publication. Both disclose relevant facts and require a safe test; trust, optional workshop scene and final repair support differ.
**Exit:** test procedure agreed. The original bell blueprint contains inward and neighbor lines; Muling's pendant clasp matches its keeper fitting, a subtle optional visual clue, not an explicit accusation.

### E-03 — The furnace that fights back / 会反击的熔炉
**Play:** prototype test in a contained furnace; move on timed platforms, tune temperature and pressure, redirect heat vents. An echo guardian repeats the obsolete sequence and ignores corrected safety signals. Disable its copying mechanism by completing the real safe sequence while defending the operators; failed pressure resets the current stage, never injures bystanders. 烬婆 initially reaches for controls, then lets 小铆 lead.
烬婆: “压力在升——把阀交给我！” / “Pressure's rising—give me the valve!”
小铆: “我知道！先泄压，再关进料。师傅，帮我看温度！” / “I know! Vent first, then stop the feed. Watch the temperature for me!”
烬婆: “……温度稳定。你继续。” / “…Temperature steady. Keep going.”
Muling: “右边那道火是回响。真正的阀在下面！” / “The fire on the right is an echo. The real valve is below!”
**Exit:** test passes with actual evidence; workers approve deployment after hearing the result, not because player clicks continue.

### E-04 — Light the lanterns / 把灯点起来
**Play:** the repaired furnace powers a lantern procession; workers voluntarily complete 承火调. Join the festival, try the improved forge or return later. 烬婆 publicly takes responsibility and gives 小铆 co-design authority. Player physically loads pump parts for forest; receipt confirmed separately.
烬婆: “上次提前开炉，是我的决定。今天的安全规程，由我们一起签字。” / “Starting early last time was my decision. Today we sign the safety procedure together.”
小铆: “师傅，第一盏灯给你。不过它的图纸我可要留着。” / “The first lantern is yours. I'm keeping its blueprint, though.”
Player: “古铃图上那条通往邻居的线，能重新接上吗？” / “Can that line connecting the neighboring bells be restored?”
Muling: “能修线，可光修线还不够。得有人愿意回应。” / “We can repair the line. But a line isn't enough. Someone must be willing to answer.”
**Visible payoff:** shops reopen, lanterns light one by one, pump returned; optional forging upgrades change gameplay. 
**Hook:** a grounded sky courier arrives for flight components; help deliver them to 云脊群岛, where an old route leads to a library with records of travelers. Sky returns wind data/route access only after actual exchange. The next destination advances personal search, not just repair count.

## CHAPTER IV — SKYSPINE ISLES / 第四章·风会记得路
### 逐风调 / Open Sky

**Player promise:** freedom, discovery, vertical adventure and a quieter moment with Muling. The region is not a factory-style repair mission. 远帆 introduces gliding; 翎羽 is revealed as his experienced sister when a flight becomes contested.

### S-01 — Catch the wind / 接住风
**Enter:** deliver forge flight components. Open with a gust stealing a courier pouch. The player learns a short, forgiving glide by following its ribbon, with safe landing and quick retry. Optional island vistas and wind collectibles offer route freedom.
远帆: “接住那个包！里头是去书库的信——哇，你飞得比我第一次好多了！” / “Catch that pouch! It's mail for the library—wow, you're better than I was on my first flight!”
Player: “风还会把我吹回去？真好玩。” / “The wind can carry me back too? That's fun.”
翎羽: “远帆，离开外侧风道。那条路不能飞。” / “Yuanfan, stay away from the outer wind channel. That route is closed.”
远帆: “我画了新航图。你看都没看。” / “I drew a new route. You haven't even looked.”
**Exit:** deliver components, practice safe glide, choose lead flight or joint training later; actual route evidence matters. Muling looks mildly airsick but insists she is “checking the wind.”

### S-02 — The wind recorder / 风中的旧记录
**Play:** explore islands via wind patterns; locate a flight recorder by matching safe routes to recovered markers. Reconstruct old accident through three environmental clues, then a playable model glider simulation; no actual sibling harm replay necessary. 远帆 cut cargo line to save both after 翎羽 misjudged wind. 
翎羽: “那天是我判断错了风。我一直以为是你太冒险。” / “I read the wind wrong that day. I kept telling myself you were reckless.”
远帆: “我割掉货绳，是为了让我们都能飞回去。” / “I cut the cargo rope so both of us could fly home.”
Player: “这张图能证明他现在知道怎么选路。要不要试一条安全的？” / “This chart shows he can choose a route now. Shall we test a safe one?”
**Choice:** let 远帆 lead or request a shared training route; both respect his agency and produce distinct final aerial support. Unlock optional high ledge after wind training, including a rare cat that reacts to glider noise.

### S-03 — A letter in the clouds / 云上的信
**Play:** deliver a real message to an isolated island, not retrieve arbitrary objects. Route switches respond to wind; player and Muling coordinate updraft markers; 远帆 leads or shares flight according to choice; 翎羽 flies beside him. An echo mimics old wind flags and false directions; read actual wind, dodge drifting shapes, escort the courier through safe air lanes. No damage-only success, no mandatory fast reaction without accessible timing alternative.
远帆: “姐姐，右边有上升气流，我走右边！” / “Sis, there's an updraft on the right. I'm taking it!”
翎羽: “收到。我跟着你，不替你选。” / “Understood. I'm following you, not choosing for you.”
Muling: “等一下——我的围巾在飞，不是我在飞！” / “Wait—my scarf is flying, not me!”
**Exit:** letter reaches recipient; player sees the isolated island reconnect. Earn wind traversal with partner assistance and safe landing. 

### S-04 — A cup for someone missing / 留着的那杯茶
**Enter:** optional exploration before a required short quiet story beat, beside a cloud overlook. Player and Muling can sit, collect a feather, watch cloud shadows. She sets out two cups and fixes a damaged old pendant; do not display any historic protagonist image.
Player: “你旅行这么久，是在找什么吗？” / “You've traveled a long time. Are you looking for something?”
