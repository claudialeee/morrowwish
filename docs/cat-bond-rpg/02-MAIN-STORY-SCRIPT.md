# CAT BOND — THE LAST RESONANCE
## Main Story Script · Scene-by-scene draft · Canon 1.0 · 2026-11-24

This script follows the canonical rules in 01-NARRATIVE-BIBLE.md. It covers the complete main-campaign dramatic spine and all critical dialogue/reveal scenes. Optional side quests, ambient barks, combat barks, and every individual NPC schedule line are separate writing passes; they must not contradict this script.

## PROLOGUE — THE SILENT BELL

### P-01 — Six Sanctuaries
**Location:** Six-region montage, then the Central Star Bell.  
**Purpose:** Establish the scale of the world and the catastrophe without an exposition lecture.  
**Visual:** Six distinct sanctuaries answer one another in sequence. The camera follows each melody toward the central Star Bell. A falling star strikes the central bell; its tongue breaks. The central note repeats. The six sanctuaries fracture and go dark. Cats begin forgetting familiar faces and routes. The Hollow Echo appears as a distortion in reflections, not as a speaking villain.

**Sound:** Six distinct musical motifs become one repeating, unresolved note.

### P-02 — The First Bellkeeper's Choice
**Location:** Central Star Bell, during the catastrophe.  
**Purpose:** Establish that the player's sacrifice was voluntary and that Muling witnessed it.

Muling: “还有办法。我们还没试完所有办法。”  
Player: “来不及了。”  
Muling: “那就再找一天。”  
Player: “它已经进入港口了。再等下去，连他们是谁都不会记得。”  
Muling: “那就让我去。”  
Player: “你去了也一样。”  
Muling: “你答应过我，不会再一个人做这种决定。”  
Player: “我知道。”  
Muling: “那就回答我。你听见了吗？”  
Player: “听见了。”  
Muling: “看着我。”  
Player: “对不起。”  
Muling: “我不要你的道歉。”  
Player: “如果他们还能回应彼此，就让他们自己发出声音。”  
Muling: “你回来以后，亲自告诉我这句话是什么意思。”

The player places a paw on the broken Star Bell. Their name and autobiographical memories enter the emergency anchor chamber. Muling reaches for the player, but the seal closes between them. She sees the sacrifice clearly.

### P-03 — Years of Waiting
**Location:** Sunlit Garden, years later.  
**Purpose:** Show Muling's grief and independent work without explaining the full plan.  
**Visual:** Seasons pass over the abandoned garden. Muling returns with maps, old records, tools, and broken bells. She studies the six sanctuaries and gradually loosens the emergency seal.

No dialogue. The old bell gives one imperfect note.

### P-04 — Awakening
**Location:** Sunlit Garden.  
**Purpose:** Introduce the player and Muling's new identity as a traveler.

Player: “这里是……？”  
Muling: “阳光花园。”  
Player: “你认识我？”  
Muling: “我叫暮铃。你呢？”  
Player: “我不知道。”  
Muling: “那就先不找名字。名字不会跑。”  
Player: “这里发生过什么？”  
Muling: “很久以前，有一座钟停了。现在它又开始出声了。”  
Player: “为什么？”  
Muling: “这正是我想查清楚的事。”

The old bell reacts to the player's presence.

### P-05 — The Journey
Muling explains that cats across six regions are losing memories. She asks the player to help restore the sanctuaries.

Player: “你一个人不行吗？”  
Muling: “如果我一个人行，我就不会站在这里了。”  
Player: “那我们一起去。”  
Muling: “你甚至不知道自己是谁。”  
Player: “但我知道他们需要帮助。”  
Muling, quietly: “……还是没变。”

**Player objective:** Leave Sunlit Garden and travel to Misttide Harbor.

## CHAPTER I — MISTTIDE HARBOR / 霁潮港
### The Song of Returning Tides · 归潮调

### H-01 — Arrival in the Fog
**Purpose:** Introduce the first regional crisis and 栖蓝.

The harbor is covered in sea fog. Cats recognize their boats but not the people who crew them. 栖蓝 is trying to reopen the route.

栖蓝: “你们是来修灯塔的吗？”  
Muling: “如果灯塔愿意配合的话。”  
栖蓝: “灯塔又不会说话。”  
Muling: “那就比某些人好沟通。”  
栖蓝: “你是在说我吗？”  
Muling: “我可没点名。”

栖蓝 asks the player to help restore the lighthouse.

### H-02 — The Keeper Who Refuses
**Purpose:** Introduce 岬灯 and his fear.

岬灯: “灯亮了，船就会回来。可有些船不应该再出海。”  
栖蓝: “你又在说以前的事情。”  
岬灯: “你根本不知道以前发生了什么。”  
栖蓝: “那你倒是告诉我啊！”  
岬灯: “我不想再看见有人因为一盏灯而受伤。”

The player chooses whether to comfort 栖蓝 first or ask 岬灯 about the old storm. This changes early trust and later support, but both paths lead to the lighthouse investigation.

### H-03 — The Flooded Logbook
**Purpose:** Reveal the past without making either NPC a villain.

The player explores the flooded lighthouse basement, follows tide marks, and recovers the old rescue log. 岬灯 had closed the lighthouse during a storm; the rescue vessel lost its bearing but the crew survived. 岬灯 has spent years blaming himself.

岬灯: “我以为关掉灯，就能保护他们。”  
栖蓝: “你保护不了所有人。”  
岬灯: “我知道。”  
栖蓝: “那你为什么还不肯让我自己试一次？”  
岬灯: “因为我不想再失去任何人。”  
栖蓝: “我也不想。但我不能一辈子待在港口。”

### H-04 — Storm Rescue
**Purpose:** Turn the relationship through action.

A storm arrives before the lighthouse is fully repaired. The player navigates tide gates and beam mirrors while 栖蓝 steers a rescue boat. 岬灯 must trust her and guide the beam.

岬灯: “左边的光路！栖蓝，跟着我说的做！”  
栖蓝: “这次我自己掌舵！”  
岬灯: “好。那我负责照亮你的路。”

### H-05 — The First Melody
The harbor cats gather at the lighthouse. The player unconsciously sings the First Note. The locals complete 归潮调.

The sanctuary wakes. Cats remember their companions and old routes. A supply vessel is sent toward Rootsleep Forest; the forest later answers with medicinal plants and a waterway map.

Muling quietly joins the second phrase.

Player: “你刚才唱的那一段是什么？”  
Muling: “古歌。”  
Player: “你以前听过？”  
Muling: “听过很多。”  
Player: “你为什么看起来难过？”  
Muling: “因为有些歌，最好不要一个人唱。”

**Clue:** Muling knows the melody more intimately than an ordinary researcher should.

**Chapter reward:** Harbor map route, lighthouse rescue support, first melody, new cats, first Catdex records.

## CHAPTER II — ROOTSLEEP FOREST / 眠根林
### The Song of Living Roots · 相枝调

### R-01 — Two Forests
**Purpose:** Present a conflict that cannot be solved by defeating an enemy.

The upper-canopy community and underground root keepers accuse each other of stealing water. 芽禾 asks the player to find a missing medicine shipment. 榆川 blocks access to the root tunnels.

芽禾: “他们把水全拿走了！”  
榆川: “我们每天都在维护根系，你们却从来不问水去了哪里。”  
芽禾: “那你为什么不说？”  
榆川: “因为你们从来没有问过。”

### R-02 — The Scent Trail
**Gameplay:** Follow medicine scents through canopy paths and underground root tunnels. The player finds that the missing medicine was diverted to treat underground root sickness.

The player discovers 榆川 has secretly maintained a water route to protect 芽禾's gardens.

榆川: “我以为只要水还在，他们就会明白。”  
芽禾: “我只看见水越来越少。”  
榆川: “我忘了告诉你们，水被送到了哪里。”  
芽禾: “你不能总是替别人决定什么是最好的。”

### R-03 — The Root Collapse
**Purpose:** Make the NPCs solve the crisis together.

A major root tunnel collapses. The player must operate a paired-cat mechanism: one cat navigates the upper channels while another opens underground gates. 芽禾 and 榆川 coordinate water flow.

芽禾: “我不想再靠猜测知道你们在做什么。”  
榆川: “那就把水量、时间和路线都写下来。”  
芽禾: “每个月一起检查。”  
榆川: “成交。”

### R-04 — The Second Melody
The two communities complete 相枝调 together. The forest sanctuary reopens.

Muling repairs a hidden routing mechanism. The player notices two paths engraved in the stone.

Player: “这里有两种方向。”  
Muling: “嗯。”  
Player: “为什么？”  
Muling: “中央星铃需要听见六座圣所。先让声音传回中央，才能判断它们是否恢复。”  
Player: “另一条路呢？”  
Muling: “旧路。断了很久。”

**Clue:** She has an answer ready before the player finishes asking.

The forest sends cooling water and medicinal herbs to Emberforge. Emberforge later answers with tools and pumps.

**Chapter reward:** Root tunnels, scent tracking, paired traversal, second melody, cross-region supply route.

## CHAPTER III — EMBERFORGE CITY / 赤炉环城
### The Song of Ember · 承火调

### E-01 — The Silent Furnace
**Purpose:** Introduce 烬婆 and 小铆.

The city's central furnace has stopped. 小铆 wants to use a new safety design. 烬婆 insists on restoring the old design first.

小铆: “师傅说机器坏了可以修，人心坏了就不一定。”  
Muling: “听起来她很会修机器。”  
小铆: “她也很会骂人。”  
Muling: “那倒是挺有精神的。”

### E-02 — The Hidden Blueprint
**Gameplay:** Explore the old forge, collect parts, regulate temperature, and reconstruct a damaged safety diagram.

The player discovers 烬婆 shortened a safety test to meet a festival deadline. The resulting accident injured two workers.

小铆: “你教我所有东西，为什么唯独不让我看这张图？”  
烬婆: “因为我不想让你变成我这样。”  
小铆: “我不需要你永远正确。我需要你相信我能知道真相。”

### E-03 — The Furnace Test
The player and 小铆 test the new design. A mechanical guardian activates and the team must control heat and pressure in real time. 烬婆 initially tries to take over, then lets 小铆 finish the repair.

烬婆: “这次要是出了问题，责任算我的。”  
小铆: “不。设计是我们一起做的。责任也一起承担。”

### E-04 — The Third Melody
烬婆 publicly admits the old accident and gives 小铆 authority to implement the safer design. The workers complete 承火调 as the furnace restarts.

The sanctuary blueprint clearly shows inward and neighbor paths.

Player: “既然另一条存在，为什么不用？”  
Muling: “因为它断了。”  
Player: “不能修吗？”  
Muling: “理论上可以。”  
Player: “那我们为什么不试？”  
Muling: “因为修好一条路，和让路的另一头有人回应，是两回事。”

This is true and becomes the central philosophical conflict.

Emberforge builds wind vanes and flight components for Skyspine Isles. The isles return wind data and an airship route.

**Chapter reward:** Forging, temperature control, mechanical puzzles, third melody, improved equipment.

## CHAPTER IV — SKYSPINE ISLES / 云脊群岛
### The Song of the Open Sky · 逐风调

### S-01 — The Broken Air Route
**Purpose:** Introduce 翎羽 and 远帆.

The air routes are closed. 远帆 wants to become a courier; 翎羽 refuses to let him fly alone.

远帆: “我已经不是小孩子了。”  
翎羽: “我知道。”  
远帆: “那你为什么还要替我决定？”  
翎羽: “因为我知道飞出去以后会发生什么。”

### S-02 — The Flight Record
**Gameplay:** Glide between islands, read wind patterns, and recover a damaged flight recorder.

The old accident reveals that 远帆 cut a cargo line to save both siblings after 翎羽 misjudged the wind. 翎羽 has mistaken protection for control.

翎羽: “我以为只要不让你飞，就不会再出事。”  
远帆: “可我已经学会了飞。”  
翎羽: “我知道。只是我一直没敢承认。”

### S-03 — The Joint Flight
The player and siblings restore the route. 翎羽 flies beside 远帆 without dictating his route.

翎羽: “这次我不会替你选路线。”  
远帆: “那你跟着我吗？”  
翎羽: “当然。我又没说不陪你。”

### S-04 — Muling's Unsent Answer
**Purpose:** Deepen Muling before the reveal.

The player finds Muling repairing a broken bell pendant.

Player: “你为什么总收集这些坏掉的东西？”  
Muling: “因为它们曾经有用。”  
Player: “修好了呢？”  
Muling: “就可以继续响。”  
Player: “如果修不好？”  
Muling: “那就留下来。”  
Player: “你在等谁？”  
Muling: “一个我以为会回来的家伙。”  
Player: “他没有回来？”  
Muling: “没有。”  
Player: “那你还等吗？”  
Muling: “……我不知道。”

Muling taps the pendant twice.

### S-05 — The Fourth Melody
Couriers across the islands complete 逐风调. The sky sanctuary reopens.

The islands establish a courier route to Moonmirror Library. The library sends old flight maps and archived route records.

**Chapter reward:** Gliding, wind navigation, aerial combat, fourth melody, courier route.

## CHAPTER V — MOONMIRROR LIBRARY / 月镜书库
### The Song of Reflections · 照影调

### M-01 — The Sealed Archive
**Purpose:** Introduce 墨页 and 白栞.

白栞 wants to restore the complete catastrophe archive. 墨页 refuses to open sealed records.

墨页: “不是所有真相都适合一次性公开。”  
白栞: “不公开，错误就会继续发生。”  
墨页: “有些记录会伤害还活着的人。”  
白栞: “被隐藏的记录也会。”

### M-02 — The Mirror Rooms
**Gameplay:** Navigate mirrored rooms, reconstruct fragmented records, distinguish original memories from Hollow Echo copies.

The player finds a mural of two cats beside the Star Bell: the player and Muling.

### M-03 — The Identity Reveal
The player confronts Muling.

Player: “壁画上的那只猫……是你？”  
Muling: “是。”  
Player: “你认识我？”  
Muling: “从你还会把铃铛挂反的时候就认识。”  
Player: “那你为什么一直不说？”  
Muling: “因为我害怕。”  
Player: “怕什么？”  
Muling: “怕你想起一切之后，再一次做出那个决定。”  
Player: “我当年做了什么？”  
Muling: “你把自己留在了星铃里。”  
Player: “你看着我这么做？”  
Muling: “是。”  
Player: “你为什么不拦住我？”  
Muling: “我拦了。”  
Muling: “我没能拦住。”

### M-04 — The Betrayal Reveal
白栞 restores a routing record. It shows that each repaired sanctuary has been set toward the center.

Player: “六地的记忆危机，是你造成的？”  
Muling: “是我让封印逐渐松动。”  
Player: “你知道会发生什么？”  
Muling: “我知道记忆会开始流失。我以为圣所恢复后就能修复。”  
Player: “你把所有人置于危险之中。”  
Muling: “我知道。”  
Player: “为了让我回来？”  
Muling: “是。”

Muling does not deny her actions or blame the player.

### M-05 — The Two Routes
墨页 and 白栞 reveal the two original sound paths and the emergency anchor.

Player: “如果走中央路线，他们都会失去记忆？”  
Muling: “是。”  
Player: “包括你？”  
Muling: “包括我。”  
Player: “你还准备这么做？”  
Muling: “我没有找到更可靠的办法。”  
Player: “你不能替他们决定。”  
Muling: “我知道你会这么说。”

Muling leaves for Starfall Expanse.

The library sends the complete star chart and historic coordinates to Starfall.

**Chapter reward:** Mirror navigation, record reconstruction, fifth melody, full identity reveal, full betrayal reveal.

## CHAPTER VI — STARFALL EXPANSE / 星坠原
### The Song Beyond Stars · 续星调

### F-01 — The Central-Control Dispute
**Purpose:** Mirror the Muling/player conflict through 星芽 and 砾川.

星芽 wants to rebuild a single central controller. 砾川 argues that the six regions must share responsibility.

星芽: “分散决策只会让错误变多。”  
砾川: “把所有决定交给一个人，只会让错误变得更大。”  
星芽: “你根本不相信系统。”  
砾川: “我不相信没有人能回应的系统。”

### F-02 — The Starfall Record
**Gameplay:** Navigate the crater, align star maps, avoid Hollow Echo illusions, and restore the observatory.

The complete record shows the fallen star, the broken bell tongue, the six damaged sanctuaries, and the player's willing sacrifice.

The player remembers the choice.

### F-03 — Muling's Plan
Muling appears at the central ruin.

Muling: “我知道你已经看见了。”  
Player: “你准备把所有猫咪的记忆送进中央星铃。”  
Muling: “是。”  
Player: “让他们失去自己的一生，只为了让我回来？”  
Muling: “我不想让你永远留在那个地方。”  
Player: “我当年选择了这么做。”  
Muling: “我知道。”  
Player: “你不尊重我的选择。”  
Muling: “我尊重过。然后我等了这么多年。”  
Player: “所以你决定替所有人做选择？”  
Muling: “我愿意承担代价。”  
Player: “你不能替他们承担他们自己的记忆。”  
Muling: “如果我不这么做，最后所有人都会失去一切。”  
Player: “你怎么知道？”  
Muling: “因为我已经找了这么多年。”  
Player: “你只是再也不相信他们能做到。”

### F-04 — The Ring Becomes Possible
The player presents the six regional exchanges. Each neighboring region has sent and received something meaningful. The six routes are no longer empty mechanisms.

Player: “你说他们不能互相回应。”  
Muling: “一次回应不代表永远稳定。”  
Player: “那就让他们继续回应。”  
Muling: “你还是和以前一样。”  
Player: “不一样了。这次我不是一个人。”

星芽 accepts distributed control. 砾川 reopens the route to Harbor. The six regions complete their reciprocal exchange.

### F-05 — The Last Inward Call
Muling sets all six sanctuaries to inward mode. The Hollow Echo spreads toward the central bell.

Muling: “跟我回来。”  
Player: “我已经回来了。”  
Muling: “那就跟我回到以前。”  
Player: “以前已经过去了。”

The final battle begins.

## FINALE — THE LAST RESONANCE

### B-01 — Phase One: Muling, the Reverse Bellkeeper
**Battle purpose:** A personal confrontation, not a generic villain fight.

Muling uses reversed melodies, inward sound chains, familiar duo patterns, and bell pulses. The player disables the inward gates while protecting the regional NPCs.

Muling: “你为什么总是先看别人？”  
Player: “因为他们就在我身边。”  
Muling: “这次看着我！”  
Player: “我一直在看你。”  
Player: “也看见了你做过的事。”

Muling's attacks become less controlled as the player breaks the inward paths. She is fighting to finish the ritual, not to kill the player.

### B-02 — Phase Two: The Hollow Echo
**Battle purpose:** Turn the theme into a combat mechanic.

The Hollow Echo copies the player, Muling, and the collected cats. The player uses Catdex to identify habits and records that the copies cannot reproduce. Duo Bond interrupts mismatched reactions. Duo Promises enable special coordinated attacks.

Example encounter:
- The Echo copies Muling's appearance.
- It repeats her words but taps the pendant at the wrong moment.
- The player recognizes the mismatch.
- The real Muling warns the player: “别看它的脸！看它做了什么！”

### B-03 — Phase Three: Six Notes Return
**Battle purpose:** Make every region and game system matter.

The six NPC groups operate their sanctuary mechanisms in the environment. Each completed action changes the battlefield. The player protects them, activates each melody, uses the collected team, and breaks the Hollow Echo's central manifestation.

Muling sees the six real replies arrive. She finally accepts that the ring is working.

Muling: “他们真的……还记得彼此。”  
Player: “他们一直都可以。”  
Muling: “我以为已经太迟了。”  
Player: “你只是没有再给他们机会。”

Muling releases the final inward lock and opens the neighbor path. This does not replace the player's work; it completes the last mechanism only she can access.

### B-04 — The Final Note
The six melodies complete the ring. The Star Bell returns to its coordinating role. The emergency anchor opens and the player's memories return. The Hollow Echo loses its source of unanswered repetition and dissolves.

Muling: “我以为……只要你回来就好了。”  
Player: “我回来了。”  
Muling: “可是我做了那么多错事。”  
Player: “我知道。”  
Muling: “你还愿意让我留下吗？”  
Player: “这次，我们一起决定以后怎么走。”

## EPILOGUE — THE BELL RINGS AGAIN

### E-01 — Six Regions Answer
Cinematic montage: Harbor ships reach the forest; the forest supplies Emberforge; the forge supports the sky routes; the sky couriers reach the library; the library shares records with Starfall; Starfall's navigation beacon returns to Harbor.

Show the named NPCs living with the consequences of their choices. Do not reduce the epilogue to a montage of static portraits.

### E-02 — Muling Takes Responsibility
Muling publicly admits that she weakened the seal and intended to merge every cat's memories. Some NPCs are angry. Others are willing to work beside her but not ready to forgive her. She accepts this without demanding a reward.

### E-03 — Sunlit Garden
The garden is alive again. Cats from all six regions visit and settle in.

Muling: “你还记得我吗？”  
Player: “记得。”  
Muling: “也记得我做过什么？”  
Player: “记得。”  
Muling: “那你还愿意让我留下？”  
Player: “留下来吧。这次，我们一起决定以后怎么走。”

The player gives Muling a second garden key. The Star Bell rings normally.

**Title card:** THE BELL RINGS AGAIN.

## POSTGAME — SIX LETTERS AND A NEW HOME

1. **Muling's Six Letters:** One letter per region, revealing what she did during the years she waited. Each visit includes a real apology or repair action, not only exposition.
2. **Six-Region Reunion:** All major NPCs return to Sunlit Garden. Dialogue, activities, decorations, and small scenes vary with player choices.
3. **Star Bell Ruins:** Optional challenge dungeon, high-difficulty battles, cat collection, and Duo Promise challenges. No postgame content may reverse the Happy Ending or claim another sacrifice is required.
