# CAT BOND — THE LAST RESONANCE
## Main Story Script · Scene-by-scene draft · Canon 1.1 · player-first narrative revision

This script follows the canonical rules in 01-NARRATIVE-BIBLE.md. It covers the complete main-campaign dramatic spine and all critical dialogue/reveal scenes. Optional side quests, ambient barks, combat barks, and every individual NPC schedule line are separate writing passes; they must not contradict this script.

## PROLOGUE — A NAME TO FIND / 序章·寻找名字

**Presentation lock:** no opening animation, historical montage, flashback, mandatory slideshow, or image of the former Bellkeeper and Muling together. Show one optional text overlay on the live garden, dismissible immediately; then give control. History is not a playable flashback until Chapter V/VI. The following Chinese/English pairs are authored opening dialogue; gameplay directions are not dialogue.

### P-01 — The world, in a few lines / 世界的背景
**Optional text over live scene (no character images):**
- ZH: 很久以前，六片土地由一座古老的星铃守护。一场灾难击坏了星铃，猫咪们开始忘记熟悉的地方、朋友，甚至自己的名字。灾难曾经平息，生活也渐渐恢复。直到最近，失忆的现象再次出现。而今天，你在一座陌生的花园里醒来。
- EN: Long ago, an ancient Star Bell watched over six lands. A disaster damaged the bell, and cats began forgetting familiar places, friends, even their own names. The crisis subsided and life returned to normal. Recently, the forgetting began again. Today, you wake in an unfamiliar garden.

**Action:** dismiss or skip once; control immediately. No cinematic completion or mandatory lore reading.

### P-02 — A stranger in the garden / 独自醒来
**Action:** fixed golden-shaded cat wakes alone in Sunlit Garden. No companion or companion HUD. Move freely; chase a butterfly, investigate flowers, find a shell, hear a faint bell. Show a small current objective, not a compulsory chain of tutorial dialogs.
Player: “这里是哪里？……我的名字呢？怎么一点也想不起来？” / “Where am I? …My name? Why can't I remember it?”
Player: “先看看附近有没有认识我的猫吧。” / “I'd better see if anyone nearby recognizes me.”

Optional sign, **阳光花园·旧铃纪念碑 / Sunlit Garden · Memorial Bell**:
“多年前，一颗坠落的星辰击坏了星铃。许多猫咪因此忘记了家园和亲友。愿每一只迷路的猫，都能找到回家的路。” / “Years ago, a falling star damaged the Star Bell. Many cats forgot their homes and loved ones. May every lost cat find its way home.”
Inspecting the old bell makes it answer once; no identity label or explanation. Player can leave without inspecting it. The garden's exact historical ownership remains hidden.

### P-03 — A map on the wind / 风中的航图
**Action:** a windblown map catches on a low branch; an unfamiliar pale silver-shaded traveling cat is trying to retrieve it. The player can help by approaching and freeing the map while she steadies the branch. She introduces herself only after the event. If ignored, preserve the opportunity to meet at the harbor.
Stranger: “等等！那张图可不能拿去做鸟窝！” / “Wait! That map is not nesting material!”
Player: “这是你的？” / “Is this yours?”
Muling: “谢啦。叫我暮铃吧。我是个到处找古铃、修旧东西的旅行者。” / “Thanks. I'm Muling. I travel around looking for old bells and fixing things.”
Player: “你认识这附近吗？我连自己的名字都记不起来。” / “Do you know this area? I can't even remember my name.”
Muling: “最近不只你这样。霁潮港也有猫突然忘了熟悉的人和路。那里有座古铃，我正准备去看看。” / “You're not the only one lately. Cats in Misttide Harbor have been forgetting people and roads they know. There's an old bell there I'm going to investigate.”
Muling: “我得先去还一件工具。港口见——如果你也想去的话。” / “I need to return a tool first. See you at the harbor, if you're heading that way.”
**Exit:** she leaves independently. No recruitment, forced intimacy or plot-dependent reward for optional help.

### P-04 — The road to the harbor / 去港口
**Action:** player can explore garden and its surroundings before taking the path. An unreachable crevice and unusual distant light are optional future exploration hooks, not mandatory tasks. A sign points to the harbor; a passing cat mentions the lighthouse trouble. Optional map marker: 去霁潮港，寻找认识我的猫 / Visit Misttide Harbor and look for someone who knows me.
Player: “也许那里能找到我的过去。先去看看吧。” / “Maybe I'll find something about my past there. I'll go and see.”
If P-03 was skipped, the harbor encounter introduces Muling naturally; never pretend the player already knows her.

### P-05 — At the harbor gate / 港口入口
**Action:** arrive independently. If the map encounter occurred, Muling recognizes the player from that meeting; otherwise she introduces herself as a traveling researcher using P-03's public identity lines. Do not show evidence of their older relationship. She is here for the broken bell and the player is here for a personal lead. They are not yet bonded.
Muling (met before): “航图没再飞走，算是个好兆头。” / “The map hasn't flown away again. That's a good sign.”
Muling (not met): “你好，我叫暮铃。来调查港口那座坏掉的古铃。” / “Hello, I'm Muling. I'm here to investigate the harbor's damaged old bell.”
**Exit:** player explores the harbor; H-01 contains the incident and invitation to temporary cooperation. No companion state until accepted. A refusal permits continued exploration and later acceptance without replaying the incident.

## CHAPTER I — MISTTIDE HARBOR / 霁潮港
### The Song of Returning Tides · 归潮调

### H-01 — A boat without a way home / 找不到家的船
**Purpose:** make forgetting visible before explaining it. The harbor is busy, warm, and foggy. Keep 栖蓝 as the primary local guide; 岬灯 appears at the lighthouse, not in the opening introduction. An unnamed fisher is an ambient role, not a new major NPC.
Fisher: “船是我的……可我家在哪条街？我怎么走了三遍都找不到？” / “It's my boat… but which street is home? I've walked past here three times.”
栖蓝: “您每天都把船停在这儿。我送您回去。等一下——外面那艘船也在打转！” / “You dock here every day. I'll walk you home. Wait—that boat out there is circling too!”
Muling: “星铃坏了以后，这种失忆曾经发生过。最近又开始了。港口的古铃或许能帮我们查清原因。” / “This kind of forgetting happened after the Star Bell broke. It's happening again. The harbor's old bell may help us find out why.”
Player: “我也想知道自己为什么失忆。先帮那艘船吧。” / “I want to know why I've lost my memories too. Let's help that boat first.”
Muling: “我能固定绳子，你来找海浪的间隙。要不要搭把手？” / “I can hold the rope while you watch for a gap in the waves. Want to team up?”
**Choice:** accept temporary cooperation, or explore first and return. She waits at the harbor if declined. Never block optional exploration. Introduce 栖蓝 as navigator through what she does, not a biography.

### H-02 — The keeper's warning / 不肯点灯的守望者
**Action:** inspect the lighthouse. 岬灯 refuses a premature relight because a broken guide system can mislead boats. 栖蓝 has practiced navigation and wants to try. Player sees a dark beam and a vessel drifting offshore.
岬灯: “别碰开关。光路没校准，亮错方向比没灯还危险。” / “Don't touch that switch. Until the beam is aligned, a false light is worse than no light.”
栖蓝: “那就校准它！我读得懂潮汐表。” / “Then let's align it! I can read the tide charts.”
岬灯: “上次我也以为来得及。” / “Last time I thought we had time, too.”
**Choice:** prioritize immediate shore rescue or initial lighthouse calibration. Both preserve safety and lead to the basement; trust and later support differ (see Bible §8). No arbitrary timed narrative deadline.

### H-03 — The flooded logbook / 泡湿的航海日志
**Action:** follow tide marks into the flooded basement; use tide timing and a real paired action to recover the logbook and align mirrors. Discover optional shells/hidden cat away from the main route. No click-to-complete.
岬灯: “当年我关了灯，以为这样能让船别出港。可救援船已经在海上。我让他们失去了方向。” / “I shut off the light to keep boats from leaving. But the rescue boat was already at sea. I took away its bearings.”
栖蓝: “那这次别替我决定能不能出海。告诉我哪里危险，我来选路线。” / “Then don't decide for me this time. Tell me where the danger is. I'll choose the route.”
岬灯: “……先把你的航图给我看看。” / “…Let me see your chart first.”
Muling privately checks the bell housing only after the group has cleared it. It is ordinary repair work on first viewing.

### H-04 — The rescue and the mist / 风浪中的救援
**Action:** secure the line together, then retrieve it in wave gaps; align the beam while 栖蓝 pilots and 岬灯 guides. A Hollow Echo manifestation imitates a ship's bell to lure the vessel toward rocks. Player uses movement, dodge, and two light controls to expose it; ordinary combat cannot alone repair the sanctuary. Retry safely after failure; no permanent loss. Muling assists by holding one mechanism and following through on openings. Show mastery through animation and sound, not a numerical trust pop-up.
岬灯: “左边有礁石。光路给你了，路线你选！” / “Reefs to port. The light is yours; choose your route!”
栖蓝: “收到！跟着真正的灯走！” / “Got it! Follow the real light!”
Muling: “我守这盏。你去另一边——我们一起开！” / “I'll hold this lamp. Take the other one—we'll open them together!”
**Resolution:** the boat docks safely. Muling asks to travel together for now, after the player has experienced actual cooperation.
Muling: “你找过去，我查古铃。我们的路好像正好重合。先同行一段？” / “You're looking for your past; I'm investigating the bells. Seems our paths overlap. Shall we travel together for a while?”
Player: “好。先看看这座铃能不能帮上大家。” / “Sure. Let's see if this bell can help everyone.”
**State:** temporary companion becomes established; meaningful paired ability earned here. A refusal of optional conversations does not erase successful rescue.

### H-05 — The First Melody / 归潮调
**Action:** repaired lighthouse reveals the regional sanctuary. Player instinctively begins the local melody; sailors and keepers must freely answer and finish it. Muling joins the second phrase before being prompted.
Player: “我明明没学过，怎么知道第一个音？” / “I've never learned this. How did I know the first note?”
Muling: “有些老歌，听过就很难忘。剩下的得让这里的猫来唱。” / “Some old songs are hard to forget once you've heard them. The cats here have to sing the rest.”
栖蓝: “那是归潮调！小时候每次船回港，我们都唱。” / “That's the Returning Tide! We sang it whenever boats came home.”
**Visible payoff:** fog lifts locally, a fisher recognizes a familiar route and is greeted at home; the market returns to activity. Not all individual memories are restored by one melody. Let the player walk through the changed harbor, use a newly earned paired sensing action to find a local optional clue, collect or befriend a cat and bring a souvenir to the garden. Sensing reveals traces, never restores memories by itself.
**Immediate payoff:** rescue resolved, sanctuary working, first melody earned; player has evidence that old bells and forgetting are connected.
**New question:** why did the protagonist know the first note? Why did Muling already know the answer? No forced suspicious reaction.
**Next destination:** a supply vessel carries goods and a query to Rootsleep Forest. A returning route-map and herbal shipment constitute the other side of the link only when actually received. A report of the forest's failing water channels gives both cats a concrete reason to go there.
**Chapter reward:** new routes, optional cats, Catdex, paired ability, souvenir and first melody. Garden revisit is available, not forced.

## CHAPTER II — ROOTSLEEP FOREST / 眠根林
### The Song of Living Roots · 相枝调

### R-01 — Two Forests
**Purpose:** Present a conflict that cannot be solved by defeating an enemy.

The upper-canopy community and underground root keepers accuse each other of stealing water. 芽禾 asks the player to find a missing medicine shipment. 榆川 blocks access to the root tunnels.

芽禾: “再没水，这一批药草就救不回来了。港口还在等我们的药呢！”  
榆川: “下面的根也快干了。我不能把水都放上去。”  
芽禾: “可是上周明明说好分水！”  
Player: “先找出水去了哪里。光争谁该用水，水也不会回来。”  
**Playable hook:** follow two diverging wet trails, choose which community to help first, then discover the diversion. Preserve the other side's needs; do not resolve the argument before the investigation.

### R-02 — The Scent Trail
**Gameplay:** Follow medicine scents through canopy paths and underground root tunnels. The player finds that the missing medicine was diverted to treat underground root sickness.

The player discovers 榆川 has secretly maintained a water route to protect 芽禾's gardens.

榆川: “我以为这条水路能同时照顾两边。”  
芽禾: “难怪水量和我算的不一样！”  
榆川: “是我忘了把分水的路线告诉你们。”  
芽禾: “那以后我们一起画水路图，照顾好每一棵小苗！”

### R-03 — The Root Collapse
**Purpose:** Make the NPCs solve the crisis together.

A major root tunnel collapses. The player must operate a paired-cat mechanism: one cat navigates the upper channels while another opens underground gates. 芽禾 and 榆川 coordinate water flow.

芽禾: “我们做个共同的水量记录吧！”  
榆川: “好，把水量、时间和路线都记下来。”  
芽禾: “每个月一起检查，检查完喝杯花茶！”  
榆川: “成交！”

### R-04 — The Second Melody
The two communities complete 相枝调 together. The forest sanctuary reopens.

Muling repairs a hidden routing mechanism. The player notices two paths engraved in the stone.

Player: “这里画着两条路！”  
Muling: “好眼力！”  
Player: “它们分别通往哪里？”  
Muling: “这条把六座圣所的声音送往中央星铃，能帮我们确认它们恢复了没有。”  
Player: “那另一条呢？”  
Muling: “是条很久没用的旧路。说不定以后能修好它。”

**Clue:** She has an answer ready before the player finishes asking.

The forest sends cooling water and medicinal herbs to Emberforge. Emberforge later answers with tools and pumps.

**Chapter reward:** Root tunnels, scent tracking, paired traversal, second melody, cross-region supply route.

## CHAPTER III — EMBERFORGE CITY / 赤炉环城
### The Song of Ember · 承火调

### E-01 — The Silent Furnace
**Purpose:** Introduce 烬婆 and 小铆.

The city's central furnace has stopped. 小铆 wants to use a new safety design. 烬婆 insists on restoring the old design first.

**Arrival action:** a festival workshop goes dark halfway through a demonstration; the player catches a rolling tool, then helps 小铆 test a portable prototype. The forest's pump order is waiting.  
小铆: “旧炉的阀门又卡住了。我的新设计能自动泄压。”  
烬婆: “停手。没验证过的东西不能接进主炉。”  
小铆: “可照旧图修，它还是会卡！给我一次测试的机会。”  
Player: “先在小炉上试，不拿整座城冒险。”

### E-02 — The Hidden Blueprint
**Gameplay:** Explore the old forge, collect parts, regulate temperature, and reconstruct a damaged safety diagram.

The player discovers 烬婆 shortened a safety test to meet a festival deadline. The resulting accident injured two workers.

小铆: “师傅，这张旧图纸能帮我们避免同样的问题。一起看看？”  
烬婆: “你说得对。我原本怕自己的错误影响你。”  
小铆: “我们把经验写进新图纸，下次就能做得更好！”

### E-03 — The Furnace Test
The player and 小铆 test the new design. A mechanical guardian activates and the team must control heat and pressure in real time. 烬婆 initially tries to take over, then lets 小铆 finish the repair.

烬婆: “小铆，咱们一起把每道检查做好。”  
小铆: “好！新设计是我们一起做的，测试也一起完成。”

### E-04 — The Third Melody
烬婆 publicly admits the old accident and gives 小铆 authority to implement the safer design. The workers complete 承火调 as the furnace restarts.

The sanctuary blueprint clearly shows inward and neighbor paths.

Player: “那条旧路还在，为什么不用呢？”  
Muling: “有几处断开了。”  
Player: “我们能修好吗？”  
Muling: “有可能。”  
Player: “那就试试看呀！”  
Muling: “好。不过修好路只是第一步，还得等另一头的伙伴回应。”

This is true and becomes the central philosophical conflict.

Emberforge builds wind vanes and flight components for Skyspine Isles. The isles return wind data and an airship route.

**Chapter reward:** Forging, temperature control, mechanical puzzles, third melody, improved equipment.

## CHAPTER IV — SKYSPINE ISLES / 云脊群岛
### The Song of the Open Sky · 逐风调

### S-01 — The Broken Air Route
**Purpose:** Introduce 翎羽 and 远帆.

The air routes are closed. 远帆 wants to become a courier; 翎羽 refuses to let him fly alone.

**Arrival action:** a courier bag flies off a dock. Chase it using short gusts and safe glider practice; the bag contains a delivery waiting for the library.  
远帆: “我能飞过去！我练了整整一个月。”  
翎羽: “不行。那条风道跟出事那天一模一样。”  
远帆: “可你连我的新航图都没看过。”  
Player: “先看看今天的风，再让他自己说怎么飞。”

### S-02 — The Flight Record
**Gameplay:** Glide between islands, read wind patterns, and recover a damaged flight recorder.

The old accident reveals that 远帆 cut a cargo line to save both siblings after 翎羽 misjudged the wind. 翎羽 has mistaken protection for control.

翎羽: “以前我总想把你留在安全的码头。”  
远帆: “现在我已经能自己飞啦！”  
翎羽: “是啊，你的进步比我想象的还快。”

### S-03 — The Joint Flight
The player and siblings restore the route. 翎羽 flies beside 远帆 without dictating his route.

翎羽: “这次你来选路线！”  
远帆: “你愿意和我一起飞吗？”  
翎羽: “当然！我还想看看你的新飞行技巧呢。”

### S-04 — Muling's Unsent Answer
**Purpose:** Deepen Muling before the reveal.

The player finds Muling repairing a broken bell pendant.

Player: “你怎么收集了这么多小铃铛？”  
Muling: “它们每个都有自己的故事。”  
Player: “修好了会怎样？”  
Muling: “就能继续唱歌啦。”  
Player: “要是找不到零件呢？”  
Muling: “就先给它们留个位置。”  
Player: “这个铃铛也有故事吗？”  
Muling: “是一位老朋友留下的，我一直盼着重逢。”  
Player: “你们还没见面？”  
Muling: “还没有。”  
Player: “那我们一起找找线索吧。”  
Muling: “谢谢你。有搭档真好。”

Muling taps the pendant twice.

### S-05 — The Fourth Melody
Couriers across the islands complete 逐风调. The sky sanctuary reopens.

The islands establish a courier route to Moonmirror Library. The library sends old flight maps and archived route records.

**Chapter reward:** Gliding, wind navigation, aerial combat, fourth melody, courier route.

## CHAPTER V — MOONMIRROR LIBRARY / 月镜书库
### The Song of Reflections · 照影调

### M-01 — The Sealed Archive
**Purpose:** Introduce 墨页 and 白栞.

白栞 wants to restore the complete catastrophe archive. 墨页 refuses to open sealed records.

**Arrival action:** the player follows a library invitation delivered by the sky couriers. Two versions of the same historical record disagree.  
白栞: “这里明明少了一页。不是虫蛀，是有人把它抽走了。”  
墨页: “那部分封存了。你不知道公开它会引起什么。”  
Player: “如果它能解释我为什么失忆，我至少需要知道哪些是事实。”  
白栞: “那就从能核实的碎片开始。我们不公开别人的私人记忆。”

### M-02 — The Mirror Rooms
**Gameplay:** Navigate mirrored rooms, reconstruct fragmented records, distinguish original memories from Hollow Echo copies.

The player finds a mural of two cats beside the Star Bell: the player and Muling.

### M-03 — The Identity Reveal
The player confronts Muling.

Player: “壁画上的那只猫……是你？”  
Muling: “是我。”  
Player: “你以前就认识我？”  
Muling: “当然！你以前还会把铃铛挂反呢。”  
Player: “原来我们早就是搭档了。为什么没告诉我？”  
Muling: “我一直不知道该怎么开口。”  
Player: “一路上我一直相信你。现在我想听实话，不是安慰。”  
Muling: “我怕你恢复记忆后，又一次选择留在星铃里。”  
Player: “当年到底发生了什么？”  
Muling: “你为了保护大家，把自己的记忆留在了星铃里。”  
Player: “你那时也在那里？”  
Muling: “是的，一直在。”  
Player: “你试过阻止我吗？”  
Muling: “试过。”  
Muling: “但最后，那是你自己的选择。”

### M-04 — The Betrayal Reveal
白栞 restores a routing record. It shows that each repaired sanctuary has been set toward the center.

Player: “六地的记忆危机，和你有关吗？”  
Muling: “是我逐渐松开了封印。”  
Player: “你知道记忆会受到影响？”  
Muling: “知道。我以为恢复圣所就能及时补救。”  
Player: “港口那些找不到家的人……你一开始就知道他们会有危险？我需要一点时间。”  
Muling: “是的。我会承担我做的事。”  
Player: “你是想让我回来？”  
Muling: “是。我知道有风险，还是继续做了。我以为我能赶在最坏的事情发生前修好它。我没有权利替大家决定。”

Muling does not deny her actions or blame the player.

### M-05 — The Two Routes
墨页 and 白栞 reveal the two original sound paths and the emergency anchor.

Player: “如果走中央路线，大家的记忆都会被送进去？”  
Muling: “是的。”  
Player: “连你的也一样？”  
Muling: “我的也一样。”  
Player: “我们能不能找一个让大家都保有记忆的办法？”  
Muling: “我还没找到可靠的方案。”  
Player: “那就让每个地方的伙伴一起参与。”  
Muling: “我愿意听听，但在验证之前，我还不能放弃中央方案。”

Muling leaves for Starfall Expanse.

The library sends the complete star chart and historic coordinates to Starfall.

**Chapter reward:** Mirror navigation, record reconstruction, fifth melody, full identity reveal, full betrayal reveal.

## CHAPTER VI — STARFALL EXPANSE / 星坠原
### The Song Beyond Stars · 续星调

### F-01 — The Central-Control Dispute
**Purpose:** Mirror the Muling/player conflict through 星芽 and 砾川.

星芽 wants to rebuild a single central controller. 砾川 argues that the six regions must share responsibility.

星芽: “如果各地分别校准，数据可能会不一致。”  
砾川: “那我们共享数据，一起检查不就好了？”  
星芽: “你觉得大家都能及时回应吗？”  
砾川: “我们可以先试一轮，看看哪里需要帮助。”

### F-02 — The Starfall Record
**Gameplay:** Navigate the crater, align star maps, avoid Hollow Echo illusions, and restore the observatory.

The complete record shows the fallen star, broken bell tongue, six damaged sanctuaries and the player's willing sacrifice. **Only here** show the former opening's historical sacrifice scene and Muling witnessing it, followed by brief fragments of her years of waiting if relevant. The player remembers the choice. The record is evidence after M-03/M-04, never an opening asset.

### F-03 — Muling's Plan
Muling appears at the central ruin.

Muling: “你看到星图上的计划了吧？”  
Player: “你打算把大家的记忆集中到中央星铃。”  
Muling: “是的，那是我准备的应急方案。”

## FINALE — THE LAST RESONANCE

### B-01 — Phase One: Muling, the Reverse Bellkeeper
**Battle purpose:** A personal confrontation, not a generic villain fight.

Muling uses reversed melodies, inward sound chains, familiar duo patterns, and bell pulses. The player disables the inward gates while protecting the regional NPCs.

Muling: “中央星铃已经启动。我必须先稳住它！”  
Player: “别急，我们还有六地的伙伴。”  
Muling: “我还不能解除共鸣锁，先让我完成这一步。”  
Player: “那就让我用实际行动证明，我们有更好的办法！”  
Player: “暮铃，这次我们用大家的回应找到新的办法。”

Muling's attacks become less controlled as the player breaks the inward paths. She is fighting to finish the ritual, not to kill the player.

### B-02 — Phase Two: The Hollow Echo
**Battle purpose:** Turn the theme into a combat mechanic.

The Hollow Echo copies the player, Muling, and the collected cats. The player uses Catdex to identify habits and records that the copies cannot reproduce. Duo Bond interrupts mismatched reactions. Duo Promises enable special coordinated attacks.

Example encounter:
- The Echo copies Muling's appearance.
- It repeats her words but taps the pendant at the wrong moment.
- The player recognizes the mismatch.
- The real Muling warns the player: “别只看外表，仔细听听它的节拍！”

### B-03 — Phase Three: Six Notes Return
**Battle purpose:** Make every region and game system matter.

The six NPC groups operate their sanctuary mechanisms in the environment. Each completed action changes the battlefield. The player protects them, activates each melody, uses the collected team, and breaks the Hollow Echo's central manifestation.

Muling sees the six real replies arrive. She finally accepts that the ring is working.

Muling: “听到了！他们还记得彼此！”  
Player: “是呀，大家都赶来帮忙了。”  
Muling: “原来这些回声能连成这么完整的旋律。”  
Player: “那就一起把最后一个音符补上吧！”

Muling releases the final inward lock and opens the neighbor path. This does not replace the player's work; it completes the last mechanism only she can access.

### B-04 — The Final Note
The six melodies complete the ring. The Star Bell returns to its coordinating role. The emergency anchor opens and the player's memories return. The Hollow Echo loses its source of unanswered repetition and dissolves.

Muling: “原来我一直盼着的重逢，真的来了。”  
Player: “我们又见面啦。”
