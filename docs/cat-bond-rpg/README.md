## Canon authority

- These documents supersede the former **The Last Page** story proposal in `docs/cat-bond-the-last-page-story-bible.md`. That file is retained only as a historical archive and is **not canon**.
- Do not revive its five-land structure, Catdex-memory premise, old character roster, old Muling motivations, old reversals, or old ending.
- The current canon is: the player is the First Bellkeeper; Muling (暮铃) witnessed the player's willing sacrifice; six sanctuaries and six melodies support two distinct seal configurations; Muling seeks to replace the player by merging everyone's memories; the player restores the original reciprocal network through relationships built across six regions; the ending is a complete Happy Ending with accountability and reconciliation.
- Do not invent a new sacrifice, prophecy, secret chosen victim, memory rewrite, time reversal, energy meter, or last-minute magic rule.
- Any proposed change to a locked canon fact must be presented as a contradiction and approved by the owner before implementation.

## Important implementation distinction

**These documents define the new story; they do not prove that the playable game has already been migrated to it.** Existing game code, translations, save data, and visual assets may still contain the former campaign. Do not report the new story as implemented or live until the actual game has been updated and tested against this canon.

The Canon 1.3 candidate includes runtime dialogue and objective edits, but further gameplay migration and browser acceptance are required. No production deployment.

## Required workflow for every RPG chat

1. Read this index, then all three documents.
2. State the canon facts relevant to your task before editing.
3. Keep the scene's cause-and-effect, character motivation, foreshadowing, and player objective consistent with the Bible.

## Ownership of work

- Narrative design: owns canon, continuity, character arcs, dialogue, and story acceptance.
- Game engineering: implements the approved scenes, triggers, maps, saves, combat, and UI without rewriting canon.
- Art and cinematic production: creates approved character art, environments, key art, transitions, and animation.
- QA / Supervisor: challenges continuity, actual playability, mobile/desktop presentation, localization, and the final player experience.

When in doubt, follow the new Bible and ask for clarification rather than importing details from the archived draft.
