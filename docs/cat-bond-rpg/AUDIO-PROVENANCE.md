# Cat Bond: The Last Resonance — audio provenance

New original score and environmental audio authored for Morrow Wish in this change. No outside music, recording, MIDI, soundfont, samples or copyrighted game OST is incorporated in the new score. The source is provided for use, modification and distribution as part of this game without attribution or royalty requirements. No third-party license is asserted for older assets. Existing studio-audio.js effects are retained; they are procedural game sounds already in the repository.

The new score uses offline-rendered stereo audio buffers at 24 kHz; no CDN, external API, paid generation or separate network audio downloads. Composition source is the audio asset, not pre-rendered compressed recordings. A maximum of three completed/pending scores is retained after rendering settles. Final audio quality and device/browser acceptance are required before release. No production deployment authorized by this change.
