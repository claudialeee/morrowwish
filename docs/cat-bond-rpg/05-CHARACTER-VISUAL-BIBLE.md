# CAT BOND — THE LAST RESONANCE
## 05 · Character Visual Bible
**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-11-24 · Chat 6 visual authority

The descriptions below translate the approved character biographies into visual design. They do not add story facts or change character motivations. All named characters remain cats with individual feline anatomy and expressive body language.

## 1. Player — The Unnamed First Bellkeeper

**Identity rule:** The player is the selected playable cat. Do not overwrite the player's chosen breed, fur pattern, or display name with a fixed protagonist model.

**Visual motif:** The First Note is represented by a small, worn brass half-ring clasp and a fine six-spoke engraving that appears only when the player interacts with an original sanctuary. It is a visual language for the role, not a royal emblem, prophecy mark or inherited power.

**Presentation**
- Keep the selected cat's face and body proportions visible in every scene.
- Use a practical travel mantle or harness only as an optional visual layer; it must not conceal the selected cat's coat or species.
- Early campaign: muted travel materials, repaired stitching, no ceremonial crown or hero armor.
- Memory-return scenes: warmer light and restored personal details, not a body transformation.
- Past First Bellkeeper flashback: same selected cat silhouette and recognizable coat, with the old keeper clasp and the original bell cord.
- Emotional acting: observant eyes, slightly forward ears, protective stance; avoid a permanently smiling mascot expression.

**Forbidden:** fixed white-cat protagonist, royal/chosen-one styling, a new species at the reveal, a magical forehead tattoo that replaces the canon First Note, or a costume that implies the player was born to rule.

## 2. Muling (暮铃) — The One Who Waited

**Core silhouette:** medium-tall, lean and long-limbed feline; narrow shoulders, elegant but not fragile; slightly asymmetrical ear tufts; long tail with a dark tapered tip. Her outline should be recognizable in silhouette before the pendant is visible.

**Core appearance:** deep dusk-slate coat with subtle charcoal tabby shading, smoke-silver muzzle/chest and cheek highlights, muted amber eyes. The value range is controlled and warm enough to remain approachable. Do not make her pitch-black or skeletal.

**Costume:** travel-researcher mantle in desaturated blue-green, weathered seam piping, small map pockets, one shoulder clasp and a practical repair pouch. No villain spikes, horns, skull motifs or oversized armor.

**Signature object:** an old brass bell pendant that belonged to the player. The outer bell is worn, with a narrow split-star seam; the concealed route-adjustment mechanism sits inside. It is always the same pendant.

### Muling's three continuous visual phases

| Phase | Story timing | Visual change | Must remain unchanged |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traveling researcher | P-04 through Chapter IV | mantle neat but visibly repaired; pendant mostly closed; relaxed ears; dry, observant expression | face, amber eyes, coat, ear tufts, pendant shape |
| Truth exposed | M-03 through F-04 | same costume; pendant opened to reveal the mechanism; tired eyes, tense shoulders, less guarded gaze; a few reversed resonance traces on the mantle | same body, face, fur, eye color, pendant and silhouette |
| Reverse Bellkeeper | B-01 through B-04 | mantle edges lifted by inward resonance; broken brass light-lines orbit the same pendant; fabric worn and pulled by force; expression conflicted, not monstrous | same face and body; no horns, extra limbs, skull mask, black transformation or unrelated boss creature |

**Emotional expression sheet:** guarded half-smile; dry amusement; private grief; nervous pendant tap; fear held behind control; anger without cruelty; recognition of the six replies; remorse; quiet relief.

**Acting signature:** she taps the pendant twice when nervous, checks maps repeatedly, and is competent at repairs but bad at cooking. These details should appear in small actions, not explanatory captions.

## 3. Regional NPCs — unique silhouette and design anchors

### Misttide Harbor / 霁潮港

**岬灯 — lighthouse keeper**
- Build: broad, heavy-shouldered older cat; square muzzle; one weathered ear edge.
- Coat: storm-grey with pale salt-frost markings around the brow.
- Costume: deep navy keeper coat, layered collar, brass lantern-hook belt and weathered gloves.
- Prop: a lighthouse lens tool; no captain's crown.
- Acting: guarded, protective, gaze often directed toward the horizon rather than the person speaking.

**栖蓝 — young navigator**
- Build: smaller, athletic and quick; narrow muzzle; upright ears and a long balancing tail.
- Coat: cool blue-grey and cream with a distinct pale nose bridge.
- Costume: sea-glass scarf, compact rope harness, practical short rain cape.
- Prop: folded route chart and brass compass.
- Acting: forward-leaning, bright eyes, impatient tail movements; becomes steady at the helm.

### Rootsleep Forest / 眠根林

**芽禾 — young herbalist**
- Build: compact, springy, rounded cheeks; leaf-like ear tufts.
- Coat: warm russet and honey with a cream chin.
- Costume: moss-green shoulder wrap, herb satchel, seed vials and woven wrist ties.
- Prop: pressed-leaf field journal.
- Acting: expressive ears and quick gestures; warmth should not read as childishness.

**榆川 — root keeper**
- Build: tall, grounded, thick forequarters; broad paws for tunnel work.
- Coat: deep bark-brown with muted olive undertones and subtle root-like tabby bands.
- Costume: layered bark-fiber mantle, rootwood forearm guard and water-route tags.
- Prop: carved channel gauge.
- Acting: still, patient, economical movement; long whiskers and lowered ears show concern.

### Emberforge City / 赤炉环城

**烬婆 — master craftswoman**
- Build: powerful, stocky and mature; wide cheek ruff, sturdy paws.
- Coat: dark copper tortoiseshell with soot-black shoulder markings and a pale muzzle.
- Costume: thick charcoal forge apron, copper shoulder plates, heat-worn gloves and hinged protective goggles.
- Prop: master hammer with a wrapped handle.
- Acting: decisive posture, narrowed eyes; when she admits fault, the hammer is lowered rather than raised.

**小铆 — young mechanic**
- Build: slim, compact and agile; pointed muzzle, asymmetrical ear notch and long expressive tail.
- Coat: cool silver tabby with a darker forehead stripe.
- Costume: short work jacket, tool belt, one reinforced paw glove and copper fasteners.
- Prop: modular safety regulator.
- Acting: alert side glances, quick hands, confident stance when presenting her design.

### Skyspine Isles / 云脊群岛

**翎羽 — veteran courier**
- Build: tall and aerodynamic; long legs, narrow waist, feathered ear tufts.
- Coat: pale ash-cream with cool slate points.
- Costume: indigo flight mantle, fitted wind harness, shoulder pennant and protective goggles.
- Prop: folded glider rudder.
- Acting: precise posture, wingspan-like cape movement, affection shown through staying close rather than smiling broadly.

**远帆 — aspiring courier**
- Build: lean and youthful but not kitten-like; long tail, strong hind legs.
- Coat: warm sand-gold with cream chest and a wind-swept forelock.
- Costume: ochre courier sash, lightweight flight cap, messenger tube and climbing straps.
- Prop: route cylinder with a hand-drawn map.
- Acting: restless stance, eyes tracking the sky; confidence becomes grounded after the joint flight.

### Moonmirror Library / 月镜书库

**墨页 — chief archivist**
- Build: upright, measured and slightly angular; broad brow and neat whiskers.
- Coat: black-and-ivory tuxedo pattern with a precise facial split.
- Costume: high-collared ink-blue archive robe, layered cuffs and bronze spectacles.
- Prop: key-ring of sealed archive drawers.
- Acting: restrained gestures; he avoids eye contact when withholding the record.

**白栞 — young archivist**
- Build: fine-boned and quick; slightly oversized ears, expressive eyes.
- Coat: moon-silver and white with pale lilac eyes.
- Costume: short scholar mantle, paper-blue sash, page clips and compact satchel.
- Prop: annotated mirror-map folio.
- Acting: curious forward lean; excitement appears in ear movement and fast page handling.

### Starfall Expanse / 星坠原

**星芽 — young bell mechanic**
- Build: compact, sharply defined silhouette; upright angular ears and precise paws.
- Coat: pale gold with cream underfur and a small constellation-like forehead fleck pattern.
- Costume: geometric midnight-blue engineer coat, brass measurement bands and star-map harness.
- Prop: articulated astrolabe.
- Acting: controlled, focused gaze; when she accepts shared control, her rigid stance opens.

**砾川 — caravan leader**
- Build: broad, weathered and low-centered; strong paws and a heavy tail.
- Coat: desert tawny tabby with darker cheek and shoulder stripes.
- Costume: layered sand-cloth caravan cloak, patched leather straps, dust goggles and practical wraps.
- Prop: star compass and route ledger.
- Acting: dry half-lidded expression, economical movement; trust is shown by handing over the compass.

## 4. Shared character production rules

- Each character needs front, three-quarter, side and back references before production animation.
- Each character needs at least six readable expressions and three story poses.
- Maintain eye color, muzzle shape, markings, ear shape, tail pattern, costume seams and prop proportions across all views.
- Use a character-specific palette; avoid giving multiple NPCs the same coat silhouette.
- NPC pairs must contrast in shape language as well as color: broad/narrow, still/quick, angular/soft, vertical/low.
- Do not invent species/breed labels that are not in the canon. These are design traits, not biological claims.
- The first playable character sheet is a presentation reference only; the player's chosen cat remains the in-game protagonist.

## 5. Asset completion status

This visual bible is the approved design specification. Character turnaround sheets and individual runtime portraits are separate asset deliverables and must be listed individually in the asset manifest. A written design is not a completed character illustration.
