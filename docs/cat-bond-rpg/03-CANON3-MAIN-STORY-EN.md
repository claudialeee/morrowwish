## World and rules (spoilers)

The Star Bell and six local bells protect the cats’ memories. After a falling star damaged the central bell, Hollow Echoes began copying and overwriting memories. The protagonist sealed their own memories inside the Star Bell to contain the disaster. Luna later opened the seal to wake the protagonist, causing the disaster to return.

The bells recognize the protagonist because their memories remain in the Star Bell. When the protagonist approaches, the local bell rings briefly, revealing the Hollow Echo. Defeating it restores the bell and ends the local crisis. All six restored bells suppress the central echo, creating a chance to defeat it. The protagonist did not have this power before sealing their memories.
