# Premium soundtrack review (candidate, not release-approved)

The existing 31 original recordings were mostly short sampled arrangements. Replace the 25 non-sanctuary cues with 14 licensed, editorially selected full recordings, including the existing forest candidate. The six puzzle/ceremony pieces remain original because their notes, durations, state commits and NPC responses are gameplay evidence. This is a curated external score, not an exclusive or newly commissioned original OST.

## Binding creative direction (owner feedback, 2026-11-25)
This is a cat-centered cozy fantasy adventure, not dark fantasy or a film trailer. All music must support player enjoyment even during loss, mystery and reversals. Exploration is warm, light, tuneful and curious. Favor acoustic piano, plucked strings, woodwinds, mallets and light percussion; avoid sustained ominous drones, horror stingers, brooding ostinatos, war-march percussion and oppressive choirs. Combat is energetic and heroic, not frightening. Reserve the largest orchestral climax for the final resolution only. Sad scenes may be tender, not relentlessly melancholic. The main theme and return-home reprise should evoke affection for the cats, not a sense of tragedy. Avoid slapstick, nursery-song loops and generic cheerful corporate music as well.
Hard rejection: listener rates enjoyment or emotional comfort below 4/5, or describes an exploration track as gloomy, threatening, exhausting or trailer-like. Compare both the whole recording and a 10-minute in-game session; do not merely rely on the composer's mood tags. A deliberate thematic reprise is allowed; six distinct region themes are mandatory. No imitating or using commercial game OST recordings.

## Technical gate
- All 25 non-sanctuary scene IDs must resolve to a measured local recording; all six regions distinct. Fallback must fail browser acceptance rather than be counted as premium.
- Full musical form, no arbitrary 30-second cut; 1.2-second equal-power loop seam (subjective musical continuity still must be heard).
- Stereo 44.1 kHz 192 kbps MP3 deliverables, source provenance and SHA-256 recorded. Target integrated loudness -19 LUFS, measured acceptance -21.5 to -16.5, post-encode true peak no higher than -0.9 dBTP. Source may already be lossy; transcoding does not improve its fidelity.
- Decode lazily, 32 kHz stereo on touch devices, 44.1 kHz desktop; evict inactive long buffers and retain only active/retiring tracks as needed. Check long-track memory on actual iOS and low-end mobile. Never block gameplay when audio is disabled.
- No external runtime audio calls. Existing master/music/effects sliders, puzzle ducking, pause, save switching and one-shot ceremony evidence intact. Transition must have at most two music sources during a bounded crossfade and one after it.

## Listening gate (mandatory; not satisfied by format or CI)
Listen to the full theme and three loop transitions in each of the six regions, plus opening and final battle. Compare the existing original recording and candidate at matched loudness. Score player enjoyment, emotional comfort, musical phrasing, regional fit, instrumental realism, lack of annoying repetition, transition/seam, and dialogue/SFX separation on 1–5. Minimum 4/5 in every category, no severe issue; two independent listeners including owner approval. Re-edit/replace if too melancholic, too trailer-like, too harsh, or the seam is noticeable. Verify actual desktop speakers and phone speakers/headphones, 10-minute garden/region session, battle/return and muted melody puzzles. Keep PR draft until listening and device QA pass; no automatic deployment on this branch.

The local music-credits.html page allows single-track review and documents CC BY 4.0 credits. Include source attribution in any exported trailer using these tracks. Do not claim original composition or artist endorsement. For a long-term exclusive identity commission a bespoke original score if budget permits.
